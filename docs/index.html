Everything a calculator should have been.

Most calculator apps answer 20 for 2 + 3 × 4. This one answers 14, and gives you brackets when you need to say otherwise.

Real precedence

Multiplication before addition, brackets on the main keypad, and a depth indicator you can tap to close them.

162 currencies

Search by code, name or country. Bitcoin, Ethereum and gold sit alongside the fiat. Pin the ones you use.

Works on a plane

Rates are saved the moment they arrive. A cold start with no signal still answers, and tells you how old the numbers are.

Split to the penny

Tip, tax and round-up. When 100 splits three ways, it shows you who pays the extra penny rather than losing it.

Ask in plain words

“300 dollars in naira”. “20% off 45”. “15 miles in km”. Or point the camera at a price tag.

Rate alerts

Watch a pair in the background and get told once when it crosses your target. Not once an hour, forever.

What’s new 2026-08-12

    It collects nothing. Genuinely nothing.

    Not a soft version of that claim. There is no analytics library, no advertising, no crash reporting, and no account to make.

    • Your calculations, history and settings never leave the device. Cloud backup is switched off for this app’s data on purpose.
    • The only outbound request is for exchange rates, and it carries no identifier.
    • Scanning a price runs on the device. The photo is never uploaded and never stored.
    • The source is public, so none of the above has to be taken on trust.
