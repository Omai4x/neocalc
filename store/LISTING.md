## Full description (4000 characters max)

```
NeoCalc is a calculator that also handles money — properly, and without a
network connection.

THE CALCULATOR
• Full scientific keypad: trigonometry, logs, powers, roots and factorials
• Proper operator precedence and brackets, so 2 + 3 × 4 is 14, not 20
• Memory that survives clearing the entry, degrees or radians
• A history tape you can reuse, convert, or export
• Results grouped, auto-sized, and switched to exponent form when they need it

MONEY
• 162 world currencies with searchable country flags
• Search by code, name or country: "pound", "yen" and "NGN" all work
• Bitcoin, Ethereum and gold alongside the fiat currencies
• Pin the currencies you use; they sit at the top and on a board under
  your result
• A 30-day trend chart under every pair
• Rate alerts that watch a pair in the background and tell you once when it
  crosses your target

WORKS OFFLINE
Rates are saved the moment they arrive. A cold start with no signal still gives
you an answer, and tells you plainly how old it is.

SPLIT THE BILL
Tip, tax, round-up, and an exact split to the penny — the shares always add up
to the total, and the odd penny is shown rather than lost. Or split by what each
person actually ordered.

ASK IN PLAIN WORDS
Type "300 dollars in naira", "20% off 45", or "15 miles in km". Scan a price
with the camera, or share text into the app from anywhere on your phone.

UNITS
Length, mass, temperature, area, volume, speed, data, time and more. Define your
own units too — "1 crate = 24 bottles" — and use them like any other.

ON YOUR HOME SCREEN
A widget showing your last converted pair and your pinned currencies, plus a
Quick Settings tile that opens straight into the converter.

PRIVACY
No account. No analytics. No advertising. No trackers. Your calculations,
history and settings stay on your device and are never uploaded — cloud backup
is deliberately switched off for this app's data. The only thing that leaves
your phone is a request for exchange rates, carrying no identifier. Photos you
scan are read on-device and never sent anywhere.

Free, with nothing to buy.
```

Roughly 1,900 characters, well inside the limit.

## Content rating questionnaire

- Violence: none. The arcade games are abstract shapes; Chomp and Invaders are
  1980s-style arcade play with no depiction of injury.
- Sexual content, profanity, drugs, gambling: none.
- Simulated gambling: none. No in-game currency, no wagering.
- User interaction / user-generated content: none. The app has no accounts, no
  messaging, and no way for users to see anything another user created.
- Shares location: no.
- Digital purchases: none.

Expected outcome: **PEGI 3 / ESRB Everyone**.

## Target audience

13+. The app is suitable for all ages, but declaring a child audience triggers
Families policy requirements that the camera and network features would then
have to be justified against, for no benefit.
